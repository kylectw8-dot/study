Grammar 5 (Grammar book Unit 17)

"There is / There are"

Saying that something exists.

UseWithExamples
There is / isn'ta singular or uncountable nounThere is a post office on this street. · There isn't any water in the pond.
There are / aren'ta plural nounThere are 25 fish in the aquarium. · There aren't any trees on the mountain.
Water, rice, money are uncountable → There is some water.

Check yourself

There ___ three computers in the lab.
are (plural)
There ___ some milk in the fridge.
is (uncountable)
There ___ any students in the hall. (negative)
aren't
Grammar 6 (Grammar book Unit 2)

Present Continuous vs Simple Present

Now vs habits — and stative verbs.

FormExample
Positiveam / is / are + verb-ingHe is doing his homework right now.
Negativeam not / isn't / aren't + verb-ingIt isn't raining.
QuestionAm / Is / Are + subject + verb-ing?Is the baby sleeping?
  • Use present continuous for actions happening right now, and temporary situations around now ("I am staying at a hostel during this trip").
  • Use simple present for habits and facts ("I walk to school every day").

Stative verbs — do NOT use -ing

Type of verb✔ Correct / ✘ Avoid
Senses
smell, taste, look, seem		✔ That cake tastes good.
✘ is tasting
Thoughts
know, think (opinion), believe, remember		✔ I know the answer.
✘ am knowing
Wants
want, need		✔ I need a new phone.
✘ am needing
Possession
have (own), belong		✔ Mr Lam has full confidence.
✘ is having confidence
think and have can be both: "I think he is right" (opinion) / "I am thinking of taking a course" (action). "He has a car" (own) / "They are having a holiday" (action).

Writing · Genre 2

Writing a Vlog Script

120–150 words · 12 marks.

Part (marks)What the marker looks for
Content
4 marks		All relevant ideas expressed clearly, supported with specific details
Organisation
2 marks		Well-developed paragraphs/scenes with links, connectives and sequencers (and, but, first, then)
Language
4 marks		Good range of vocabulary and sentence patterns; accurate grammar, spelling, capitals, punctuation
Features
2 marks		Correct text-type format and genre conventions

Format

FeatureWhat it doesExample
TitleShows the main topic, often an engaging questionWhat are Hong Kong schools like?
Setting in [square brackets]Where and when the scene happens[Scene 1 – in front of the school, morning]
Stage directionsActions or camera movementsJoona waves. / Walks into the music room.
Speaker's name + colonWho is speakingJoona:
DialogueThe exact words spoken by the vlogger (and interviewees)Joona: Hi everyone! Welcome to my school!
What are Hong Kong schools like?
[Scene 1 – in front of the school, morning]
Joona waves at the camera.
Joona: Hi everyone! Today I am showing you my school. There are five floors and there is a big hall next to the playground.
[Scene 2 – in the music room]
Walks into the music room.
Joona: This is my favourite room…
Use the grammar here: present continuous for what is happening on camera ("I am walking into…"), there is / are and prepositions of place to describe the school.
