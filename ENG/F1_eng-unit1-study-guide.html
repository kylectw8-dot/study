隨和的 / 悠閒的		Relaxed in manner and character; not easily worried or stressed.
confident (adj.)
有信心的 / 自信的		Feeling sure about your own abilities to do things well.

外向的 / 喜歡交際的		Friendly, energetic, and finding it easy to talk to other people.

慷慨的 / 大方的		Willing to give money, help, or time freely to others.

Grammar 1

Simple Present Tense

For habits and facts.

SubjectVerbExample
I / You / We / Theybase verbI observe · colleagues organize
He / She / It / one person or thingverb + s / esmum encourages · dad reminds · humidity makes
Questions & negativesdo / does / don't / doesn'tDo you enjoy…? · it does not show
After does / doesn't, use the base verb: "She doesn't like…" (not likes).

Check yourself

My dad ___ (remind) me to drink water.
reminds (he → verb + s)
We ___ (play) basketball on Fridays.
play (we → base verb)
___ you enjoy reading?
Do
She ___ not eat meat.
does → "She does not eat meat."
Grammar 2

Articles: a, an, the, X

Four quick rules.

ArticleUse it forExamples
aone thing starting with a consonant sounda delicious waffle · a bottle
anone thing starting with a vowel soundan umbrella · an unusually humid morning
thea specific thing both people know aboutthe living room · the MTR station · the forecast
X (no article)after possessives: my, your, his…your homework (not "the your homework")
Listen to the sound, not the letter: an hour, a uniform.

Check yourself

___ umbrella
an (vowel sound)
___ bottle
a (consonant sound)
Let's meet at ___ MTR station near school.
the (a specific place we both know)
Did you finish ___ your homework?
X — no article after "your"

Writing · Genre 1

Writing a Personal Description

120–150 words · 12 marks.

Part (marks)What the marker looks for
Content
4 marks		All relevant ideas expressed clearly, supported with specific details
Organisation
2 marks		Well-developed paragraphs/scenes with links, connectives and sequencers (and, but, first, then)
Language
4 marks		Good range of vocabulary and sentence patterns; accurate grammar, spelling, capitals, punctuation
Features
2 marks		Correct text-type format and genre conventions

Format

PartAboutWhat to write
Title~5 wordsWho it is about (e.g. Ollie King) or a short catchy title with your name
Para 1 — Background~25 wordsYour name, age, where you live, family members
Para 2 — Appearance~30 wordsOverall look, height/build, face, hair; you can compare with a family member
Para 3 — Personality~35 wordsCharacter traits (and likes/dislikes) + a concrete example showing the trait
Para 4 — Hobbies & Interests~45 wordsWhat you do in your spare time, when and how often

Useful word bank

TopicWords
Appearanceathletic, average/medium height, bushy/thin eyebrows, chubby, coarse/sleek hair, good-looking, petite, plump, rosy cheeks, slender, unkempt, well-built
Personalityambitious, calm, down-to-earth, easy-going, (in)considerate, (im)patient, (ir)responsible, modest, stubborn, trustful, (un)sociable, (un)thoughtful
Hobbiesblog writing, cafe hopping, calligraphy, collecting autographs, gardening, marathon running, martial arts, photography, playing the piano/guitar, scuba diving, volunteering, yoga
Use the grammar here: simple present for habits ("I usually practise…") and adverbs of frequency in Para 4.
