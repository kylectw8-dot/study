5.1 Energy changes

Forms of energy and how they change.

Form of energyExample
Light 光能The Sun, a light bulb
Thermal (heat) 熱能The Sun, a fire
Sound 聲能Speaking, singing
Chemical 化學能Stored in fuel and food, a match, wood
Electrical 電能From a power station or a solar cell
Kinetic 動能Anything moving: a moving car
Potential 勢能Stored by height (gravitational) or stretching (elastic), e.g. a stretched rubber band
  • Energy is measured in joules (J) 焦耳 or kilocalories (kcal) 千卡.
  • The total energy before and after a change is the same. Energy is never lost; it only changes form.

Sentence pattern

Chemical energy stored in the match is converted to light energy given out by the match.
  • Jumping up: kinetic energy of the man is converted to potential energy of the man.
  • Burning wood: chemical energy stored in the wood is converted to thermal and light energy given out by the fire.
  • Rubber band plane: (elastic) potential energy stored in the rubber band is converted to kinetic energy of the plane.
  • To fly further: stretch the rubber band more → more potential energy stored → more kinetic energy for the plane.

Efficiency 效率

Efficiency = useful energy output ÷ energy input × 100%

A bulb gives 120 J of light from 600 J of electrical energy: 120 ÷ 600 × 100% = 20%. The rest becomes thermal energy.

Check yourself

The Sun gives out ______ energy and ______ energy.
light and thermal
Fuel and food store ______ energy.
chemical
A stretched rubber band has ______ energy.
(elastic) potential
A moving car has ______ energy.
kinetic
Give two units of energy.
joule (J) and kilocalorie (kcal)
Energy change as a man jumps upwards?
Kinetic energy of the man is converted to potential energy of the man.
Efficiency of a bulb: 120 J light from 600 J electrical?
120 ÷ 600 × 100% = 20%

5.2 Heat transfer

Conduction, convection and radiation.

MethodHow it worksNeeds particles?
Conduction 傳導Heat passes from the hotter part to the colder part through a material. Objects must be in contact.Yes (mainly solids)
Convection 對流Hot liquid or gas moves upwards; colder fluid sinks to take its place (convection current).Yes (liquids and gases)
Radiation 輻射Heat travels as rays. It can pass through a vacuum 真空 (e.g. from the Sun).No
A good conductor conducts thermal energy quickly (metals). A good insulator conducts thermal energy slowly (plastic, glass, air).
Dull and dark objects are good absorbers (and radiators) of radiation. Shiny and light objects are poor absorbers.

Examples

  • Purple crystal in water heated at one side: coloured water rises above the flame, moves across the top and sinks on the other side → convection.
  • Kettle handle is plastic: a good insulator, so you will not get burnt.
  • Glass outer body is an insulator: it reduces heat loss by conduction.
  • Heating element is at the bottom: hot water rises and cold water sinks, so convection heats all the water.

Cindy’s can experiment

  • IV: type of surface (dull black / shiny silver). DV: rise in water temperature.
  • Rise: black 30 − 26 = 4 °C; silver 28 − 26 = 2 °C.
  • Not conduction: air is a poor conductor and the cans do not touch the bulb. Not convection: hot air rises upwards, not sideways to the cans.
  • Conclusion: dull black surfaces are better absorbers of radiation than shiny silver ones.

Check yourself

Conduction happens when two objects of different temperatures are in ______.
contact
In convection, hot gas moves ______ and colder gas ______.
hot gas moves upwards; colder gas sinks
Which method can transfer heat through a vacuum?
Radiation
Why is a kettle handle made of plastic?
Plastic is a good insulator, so users will not get burnt.
Is a kettle’s heating element at the top or bottom? Why?
Bottom. Hot water rises and cold water sinks, so convection heats all the water.
Black can 26 → 30 °C, silver can 26 → 28 °C. Conclusion?
Rises of 4 °C and 2 °C. Dull black surfaces are better absorbers of radiation.
