2.1 Change of states + 2.2 Water cycle

Solid, liquid, gas. How water moves around the Earth.

StateWater
Solid 固體Ice is the solid state of water.
Liquid 液體Water is the liquid state of water.
Gas 氣體Water vapour is the gas state of water.

Changes of state

ProcessFrom → ToFor water
Melting 熔化solid → liquidIce melts at 0 °C
Freezing 凝固liquid → solidWater freezes at 0 °C
Evaporation 蒸發liquid → gasHappens at any temperature
Boiling 沸騰liquid → gasWater boils at 100 °C
Condensation 凝結gas → liquidSteam turns into droplets on a cold surface
Water freezes at 0 °C. It changes state from liquid to solid.
Ice melts at 0 °C. It changes state from solid to liquid.

The water cycle 水循環

  1. Evaporation: the Sun heats water in the sea. It becomes water vapour and rises.
  2. Condensation: high up it is cold. Water vapour condenses into tiny droplets and forms clouds.
  3. Raining: the droplets join together and fall as rain.
  4. Transportation: rainwater flows in rivers back to the sea.
"Every Cloud Rains Today" → Evaporation, Condensation, Raining, Transportation.

Making "rain" in the lab

  • Put water at the bottom of the beaker. The lamp (the "Sun") heats it so it evaporates.
  • Put a metal dish with ice / iced water on top. Water vapour condenses on the cold dish and drips down like rain.
Peony put hot pebbles on top and iced water at the bottom. This is the wrong way round: cold water does not evaporate much, and a hot dish cannot make the vapour condense.

Check yourself

Water vapour is the ______ state of water.
gas
Evaporation changes a ______ into a ______.
liquid into a gas
Condensation changes a ______ into a ______.
gas into a liquid
Complete: Water freezes at ___. It changes state from ___ to ___.
Water freezes at 0 °C. It changes state from liquid to solid.
Name the four main processes of the water cycle.
Evaporation, condensation, raining, transportation
Can Peony’s set-up (hot pebbles on top, iced water below) simulate rain?
No. The iced water at the bottom will not evaporate much, and the hot dish on top cannot cool the vapour to make it condense. Water should be at the bottom and the cold dish on top.

2.3 Dissolving

Solute + solvent = solution.

WordMeaningExample (salt water)
Solute 溶質The substance that dissolvesSalt
Solvent 溶劑The liquid that does the dissolvingWater
Solution 溶液Solute + solvent mixed togetherSalt solution
Soluble 可溶的Can dissolveSalt, sugar
Insoluble 不可溶的Cannot dissolveSand, fruit pieces
Solute is the Stuff that goes in. Solvent is the liquid it goes into.

Making a solid dissolve faster (rate of dissolving 溶解速率)

  • 🔥 Use hot water (higher temperature)
  • 🥄 Stir the mixture
  • 🔨 Crush the solid into smaller pieces / powder

Getting salt back from salt water

  • Leave the solution in a dish. The water evaporates and white salt crystals are left.

Mass is kept the same

  • Mass of solution = mass of solute + mass of solvent. 1 g salt + 9 g water = 10 g solution.
  • In a 50 g beaker: total = 50 + 10 = 60 g.
  • If it later weighs 55 g, 5 g of water has evaporated. Salt does not evaporate, so it still has 1 g salt and 4 g water.
  • To prove it: evaporate all the water, then weigh the salt left on an electronic balance. It should be 1 g.

WS3

2.4 Water purification

How do we make water clean?

Impurities in natural water

TypeExample
Insoluble impurities 不溶性雜質Sand, mud
Soluble impurities 可溶性雜質Salt
Micro-organisms 微生物E. coli, Amoeba

Three methods

MethodHowRemoves
Sedimentation 沉澱Leave water to stand. Heavy bits sink.Large insoluble impurities
Filtration 過濾Pour through filter paper in a filter funnelInsoluble impurities
Distillation 蒸餾Boil the water, cool the steam in a condenserInsoluble + soluble impurities + micro-organisms
  • In filtration, the liquid that passes through is the filtrate 濾液. The solid left on the paper is the residue 殘渣.
  • In distillation, the condenser 冷凝器 cools the steam so it condenses into water. Cold water goes in at the bottom and out at the top.
  • The liquid collected is distilled water 蒸餾水.
Filtration diagram: filter funnel + folded filter paper, held by a stand, over a beaker or conical flask. Label: muddy water, filter paper, filter funnel, residue, filtrate. Use pencil and ruler.

Comparing methods (sentence pattern)

Similarity: Both distillation and filtration can remove insoluble impurities.
Difference: Distillation can remove soluble impurities but filtration cannot.

Separating salt and sand

  1. Add water to the mixture and stir so the salt dissolves.
  2. Filter the mixture. Sand stays on the filter paper as residue. Wash and dry it.
  3. Evaporate the filtrate (salt solution). Salt crystals are left.

Check yourself

Name the three types of impurities in natural water.
Insoluble impurities (sand), soluble impurities (salt), micro-organisms (E. coli).
Name the three common methods to purify water.
Sedimentation, filtration, distillation
In distillation, what does the condenser do?
It cools the steam so it condenses into water.
Where does the cold water go into the condenser?
In at the bottom, out at the top.
Give one similarity and one difference between distillation and filtration.
Similarity: Both can remove insoluble impurities. Difference: Distillation can remove soluble impurities but filtration cannot.
How do you get both sand and salt back as solids?
Add water and stir → filter (sand is the residue, dry it) → evaporate the filtrate to get salt.
