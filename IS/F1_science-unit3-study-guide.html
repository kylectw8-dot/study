3.1 Living things

What makes something alive?

Living things carry out seven vital functions 生命現象 to maintain life.

Vital functionMeaningExample
MovementMove the body or parts of itVenus flytrap closes its leaves
Respiration 呼吸Need oxygen to break down food for energyWe breathe in oxygen
SensitivityDetect and react to stimuli 刺激Sweating when hot; flytrap reacts to touch
GrowthIncrease in body size and complexityA baby grows into an adult
Reproduction 繁殖Produce offspringSeeds grow into new plants; a potato grows into a new plant
Excretion 排泄Remove waste substancesUrine, sweat; plants shed leaves
NutritionNeed foodFlytrap catches insects; mould feeds on bread
"MRS GREN" → Movement, Respiration, Sensitivity, Growth, Reproduction, Excretion, Nutrition.
A Venus flytrap is a living thing because it can move / reacts to stimuli / needs food.
A plastic bag is a non-living thing because it cannot grow / cannot reproduce.
  • The wide variety of living things is called biodiversity 生物多樣性.
  • Plants can move parts of their body (e.g. leaves turn towards light). Plants and animals share all seven functions.
  • Micro-organisms like Amoeba are too small to see with the naked eye. Use a microscope.

Check yourself

Living things need ______ to break down food for energy.
oxygen (respiration)
Falling of leaves from a tree is an example of excretion. True or false?
True. Plants remove waste by shedding leaves.
Plants cannot move. True or false?
False. Parts of plants can move, e.g. leaves turn to light.
Mr Kwan sweats when he is hot. Which vital functions?
Excretion and detecting and reacting to stimuli (answer C).
Bread mould: the number of moulds increases. Which vital function?
Reproduction
Bread mould grows on bread but not on metal. Which vital function?
Nutrition: living things need food.
Bread storage experiment: what is the independent variable?
Temperature of storage. DV: area of mould or days until mould appears. Controlled: type and size of bread, sunlight.

3.2 Grouping of living things

Classify by key features.

Scientists classify 分類 living things according to their key features 主要特徵.

Animals

  • Vertebrates 脊椎動物 have a backbone. Invertebrates 無脊椎動物 do not (e.g. octopus, crabs, jellyfish, insects).
Vertebrate groupKey featuresExamples
Fish 魚類Gills, scales, fins, live in waterShark, goldfish, seahorse
Amphibians 兩棲類Moist skin, young use gills, adults use lungsFrog, toad
Reptiles 爬行類Dry scaly skin, lungs, lay eggs with shellsSea turtle, snake
Birds 鳥類Feathers, beak, wings, lay eggsPenguin, eagle
Mammals 哺乳類Hair or fur, mammary glands (feed young milk), lungsWhale, bat, panda, monkey
Bats have wings but are mammals. Whales live in water but are mammals (lungs, not gills). Penguins cannot fly but are birds (feathers).
Penguins are classified as birds because they have feathers and a beak.

Plants

GroupVascular tissueSeedsFlowersExample
Non-vascular plants✗✗✗Moss
Seedless vascular plants✓✗✗Fern
Non-flowering seed plants✓✓✗Pine
Flowering plants✓✓✓Rose, potato
  • Vascular tissues 維管組織 transport water and food.
  • The place where a living thing lives is its habitat 生境.

WS3

3.3 Identification keys + 3.4 Biodiversity

Name living things. Protect them.

Identification key 檢索表

  • A key splits living things into two groups at each step, using one feature (e.g. with wings / without wings).
  • Follow it from the top until only one living thing is left.
Each pair must be a clear yes / no pair. To separate a dolphin from a goldfish, use with lungs / without lungs (both have backbones).

Crab key example: Two claws of similar size → ball-shaped body = soldier crab; flattened body = grapsid crab. Two claws of different sizes = fiddler crab.

Why biodiversity matters

  • Biodiversity keeps the balance of the natural environment (food chains).
  • Humans depend on living things for raw materials, food and medicine.
  • If crabs decrease, seabirds that eat them will decrease because they have less food.

Threats

ThreatExample
Habitat destructionBushfires and cutting forests → koalas, giant pandas lose food and mates
Overhunting / overexploitationShark fin soup; overfishing Napoleon wrasse
Foreign species 外來物種Apple snails compete with native species for food
PollutionSea turtles eat plastic bags thinking they are jellyfish
  • Endangered species 瀕危物種 are in danger of extinction, e.g. black rhino, eastern gorilla.
  • Conservation 保育 maintains biodiversity. Afforestation (planting trees) helps.
  • 4 Rs: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle, Replace. E.g. bring your own bag, reuse bags, recycle plastic, replace plastic with paper or cloth.
The number of seabirds will decrease because they have less food.

Check yourself

Scientists use a ______ to identify living things.
key
Living things in danger of extinction are called ______ ______.
endangered species
Apple snails in Hong Kong are an example of ______ species.
foreign
Which will NOT reduce biodiversity: habitat destruction, foreign species, afforestation, pollution?
Afforestation (planting trees)
How does habitat destruction affect koalas?
They have less food and cannot find mates to reproduce.
Jellyfish vs plastic bag: two reasons jellyfish are living.
Jellyfish can reproduce and can grow (also need food, react to stimuli) but plastic bags do not.
Jellyfish and sea turtles: which groups?
Jellyfish are invertebrates because they have no backbone. Sea turtles are reptiles because they have scales and lungs.
Two ways to save sea turtles with the 4 Rs.
Reduce plastic bags by bringing your own bag; recycle plastic; replace plastic with cloth bags.
