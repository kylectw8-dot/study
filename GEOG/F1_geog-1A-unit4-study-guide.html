4.1 What are the major urban problems in Hong Kong?

Traffic, pollution, housing and waste.

1. Traffic congestion 交通擠塞

  • Serious in the CBD, in old urban areas and at traffic bottlenecks 樽頸位 such as the entrances of the Cross-Harbour Tunnel.
  • Common during the peak hours of commuting.

2. Pollution 污染

PollutionMain source
Air pollutionPower plants, vehicles (road transport)
Land pollutionSolid waste
Water pollutionDomestic sewage 生活污水 (the largest source)
Noise pollutionRoad maintenance, traffic
Light pollutionBillboards, neon signs
The largest source of water pollution is domestic sewage, not industry. Light pollution can affect health (e.g. poor sleep).
  • Solid waste is mainly sent to landfills 堆填區. Hong Kong has three landfills, all in the New Territories (Tuen Mun, Tseung Kwan O, Ta Kwu Ling). They are soon to be full and space-consuming, and are not in urban centres.
  • Busy streets at night with many neon signs (e.g. old urban areas like Mong Kok) have light and noise pollution.

3. Housing problems

  • Housing shortage: in 2021 the average waiting time for public housing rose to 5.7 years.
  • Poor living conditions: many people live in subdivided flats 劏房, which have small living space and often lack repair and maintenance.

Check yourself

True

4.2 Urban decay in old urban areas

Why old areas become run-down.

Old urban areas 舊市區 are the areas of a city developed the earliest. In Hong Kong they are mainly on both sides of Victoria Harbour.

Urban decay 市區老化 is the worsening of urban environments. It is common in old urban areas.

  • Examples of old urban areas: Sai Ying Pun, Tai Kok Tsui, To Kwa Wan, Sham Shui Po, Mong Kok.
  • Ma On Shan is a new town, not an old urban area.

Characteristics of urban decay

  • 🏚️ Old and worn-out buildings that often lack maintenance
  • 🏘️ High building density
  • ⚠️ Serious land use conflicts 土地用途衝突: two incompatible land uses are too close, e.g. a car repair shop under flats
  • 🛠️ Facilities in poor condition
Building density in old urban areas is high, not low. The living environment is not pleasant.

Check yourself

Old urban areas in HK are mainly on both sides of ______.
Victoria Harbour
Which is NOT an old urban area: Sai Ying Pun, Tai Kok Tsui, Ma On Shan, To Kwa Wan?
Ma On Shan (a new town)
______ occurs when two incompatible land uses are too close to each other.
Land use conflict
Which are characteristics of urban decay: low density, land use conflicts, poor facilities, old buildings?
Land use conflicts, facilities in poor condition, old and worn-out buildings
An old tenement building: common in old urban areas, lacks maintenance, pleasant living? Which are correct?
(1) and (2) only (answer A)
