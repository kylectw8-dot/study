3.1 What is the CBD of Hong Kong like?

Where it is and what it is like.

A Central Business District (CBD) 中心商業區 is the major commercial area of a city.

Where is Hong Kong’s CBD?

  • The CBD of Hong Kong is Central 中環.
  • It has expanded to Sheung Wan 上環 in the west and Admiralty 金鐘 in the east.
West → East along the harbour: Sheung Wan → Central → Admiralty.

What is the CBD like?

  • 🏢 Tall and dense buildings (skyscrapers)
  • 💼 Mainly made up of commercial land use (offices, banks, shops)
  • 💰 Very high rents and land prices
  • 🚗 Heavy traffic and many people
  • 🎭 Venues for cultural activities (e.g. City Hall)
  • 🏛️ Government offices: the Government Headquarters is in Admiralty, so the CBD is also an administrative centre 行政中心.
CBD rents are high, not low. Buildings are dense, not sparse.
