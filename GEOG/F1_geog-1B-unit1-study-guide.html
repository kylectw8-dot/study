1.1 What are the major natural hazards in the world?

Types and where they happen.

Natural hazards 自然災害 are natural events that threaten human life and property.

If an event hurts nobody and damages nothing (e.g. an earthquake on an empty remote island), it is not a natural hazard.
TypeRelated toExamples
Geological hazardsThe internal structure of EarthEarthquakes, volcanic eruptions
Weather-related hazardsExtreme weather conditionsTropical cyclones, floods, droughts, landslides (after heavy rain)
  • Drought 旱災 (dry, cracked ground) is related to extreme weather and can be found all over the world.

Tropical cyclones 熱帶氣旋

  • A tropical cyclone looks like a huge spinning cloud on satellite photos (e.g. near the Philippines).
  • They mostly form between the Tropic of Cancer (23.5°N) and Tropic of Capricorn (23.5°S) and move to around 30°N and 30°S, over warm oceans.
  • Areas affected: Southeast Asia, Central America (and the south of North America), northern Australia and southeastern Africa.
  • Southeast Asian countries affected: the Philippines, Vietnam (also southern China).
