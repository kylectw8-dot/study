10A Rectangular coordinates

(x, y): across, then up.

  • A point is written (x, y): x-coordinate first (horizontal), then y-coordinate (vertical).
  • The x-coordinate of (−8, 4) is −8; the y-coordinate of (7, −18) is −18.
  • Points on the x-axis have y = 0 (e.g. (2, 0), (−8, 0)). Points on the y-axis have x = 0 (e.g. (0, −5)).
QuadrantSigns
I (top right)(+, +)
II (top left)(−, +)
III (bottom left)(−, −)
IV (bottom right)(+, −)
Points on the axes do not belong to any quadrant.

10B–C Distances and areas

Horizontal and vertical lines.

  • Horizontal line (same y): length = difference of x-coordinates. A(2, 7), B(11, 7): AB = 11 − 2 = 9.
  • Vertical line (same x): length = difference of y-coordinates. C(7, 4), D(7, −8): CD = 4 − (−8) = 12.
  • A(a, 6), B(8, 6), AB = 15 and A on the left → a = 8 − 15 = −7.

Areas

A(−3, 3), B(−3, −8), C(15, −8): AB = 11 (vertical), BC = 18 (horizontal)
Area of △ABC = ½ × 18 × 11 = 99 sq. units
  • For slanted shapes, draw a rectangle around them and subtract the outside triangles.

10D Transformations

Translate, reflect, rotate.

Transformation(x, y) becomes
Translate right a units(x + a, y)
Translate left a units(x − a, y)
Translate up b units(x, y + b)
Translate down b units(x, y − b)
Reflect in the x-axis(x, −y)
Reflect in the y-axis(−x, y)
Rotate 90° anti-clockwise about O(−y, x)
Rotate 90° clockwise about O(y, −x)
Rotate 180° about O(−x, −y)
  • A(6, 3) translated right 5 → (11, 3). B(7, 7) translated down 3 → (7, 4).
  • C(6, 5) reflected in the x-axis → (6, −5).
  • P(4, 5) rotated 90° anti-clockwise → (−5, 4). Q(2, 1) rotated 180° → (−2, −1).
Not sure? Sketch the point and turn your paper!
