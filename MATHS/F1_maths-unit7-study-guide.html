7A Percentages basics

Percent means "out of 100".

TypeMethodExample

What % is A of B?A ÷ B × 100%120 of 80 → 120/80 × 100% = 150%
  • 12% of 750 mL = 90 mL; 24% of q is 36 → q = 150; 86 cm is 43% of x → x = 200.
  • 70 teachers, 28 male → 42 female → 42/70 × 100% = 60%.

SHW 7-B1

7B Percentage change

Increase and decrease.

Percentage change = (new value − original value) ÷ original value × 100%
  • Positive → increase; negative → decrease.
  • 20 increased by 12 → 12/20 = 60% increase. 80 → 100: 20/80 = 25% increase.
  • 180 → 117: −63/180 = −35% (35% decrease). 36 → 27: −25%.

New and original values

  • Increase by r%: new = original × (1 + r%). $25 increased by 12% = 25 × 1.12 = $28.
  • Decrease by r%: new = original × (1 − r%). 240 reduced by 35% = 240 × 0.65 = 156.
  • Find the original: reduced by 30% to $840 → original × 0.7 = 840 → $1200.
Always divide by the original value, not the new one.

7C Profit, loss and discount

Shopping maths.

Profit = selling price − cost priceProfit % = profit ÷ cost price × 100%
Loss = cost price − selling priceLoss % = loss ÷ cost price × 100%
Discount = marked price − selling priceDiscount % = discount ÷ marked price × 100%
  • Cost $150, sold $195 → profit $45 → 45/150 = 30% profit.
  • Cost $300, sold $195 → loss $105 → 35% loss.
  • Cost $80, profit 35% → selling price 80 × 1.35 = $108.
  • Cost $700, loss 38% → loss $266, selling price $434.
  • Marked $120, 15% discount → 120 × 0.85 = $102.
  • Marked $780, discount $234 → 234/780 = 30%.
  • Sold for $150 at 20% profit → cost = 150 ÷ 1.2 = $125.
