2A Directed numbers and the number line

Positive and negative.

  • Positive numbers (+) are greater than 0; negative numbers (−) are less than 0. 0 is neither.
  • Directed numbers show opposite directions: +3 floors = up 3 floors, so −4 floors = down 4 floors.
  • The opposite number of −12 is +12.
  • On a number line, numbers on the right are greater.
−17 < −13  (−17 is further left)
  • Ascending order = smallest to largest: −30, −14, +17, +28.
  • Descending order = largest to smallest.
For negatives, the bigger the number after the sign, the smaller the value: −30 < −14.

2B Adding and subtracting directed numbers

Two signs together.

RuleExample
+ (+a) = + a(+27) + (+6) = 33
+ (−a) = − a(+7) + (−18) = 7 − 18 = −11
− (+a) = − a(+16) − (+31) = 16 − 31 = −15
− (−a) = + a(+11) − (−5) = 11 + 5 = 16
Same signs → +. Different signs → −.
  • −12 + 8 = −4
  • −7 − 10 = −17
  • (−17) + (−1) = −18
  • (−13) − (−12) = −13 + 12 = −1
  • (−1) − (+1) − (−1) = −1 − 1 + 1 = −1

2C Multiplying and dividing directed numbers

Count the negative signs.

SignsAnswerExample
(+) × (+)+(+3) × (+4) = 12
(−) × (−)+(−3) × (−10) = 30
(+) × (−)−(+9) × (−3) = −27
(−) ÷ (+)−(−144) ÷ (+9) = −16
An even number of negative signs → positive. An odd number → negative.
  • Follow the order of operations: (−6)(−3) − (+15) = 18 − 15 = 3.
  • (+23) − (+8) × (−4) = 23 − (−32) = 23 + 32 = 55.
