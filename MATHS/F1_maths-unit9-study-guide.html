9A Areas of plane figures

Know the formulas.

Trapezium½ × (upper base + lower base) × height
  • The height must be perpendicular to the base.
  • Shaded region = big area − unshaded area, or split a polygon into simple shapes and add.
Trapezium with bases 3 cm and 13 cm has the same area as a square of side 8 cm:
½(3 + 13)h = 64 → 8h = 64 → h = 8

9B Polyhedra and volume of prisms

Cross-section × length.

  • A polyhedron is a solid with only flat faces (cube, prism, pyramid). Spheres, cylinders and cones are not.
  • A right prism has the same cross-section all along its length.
Volume of prism = area of cross-section × length
  • Cross-section 36 cm², length 16 cm → 576 cm³.
  • Cube of side 7 cm → 7³ = 343 cm³.
  • Cuboid 28 × 17 × 12 = 5712 cm³.
  • Triangular prism: triangle base 4, height 3, length 8 → ½ × 4 × 3 × 8 = 48 cm³.
Units: area in cm², volume in cm³.

9C Total surface area of prisms

Add the areas of all faces.

Total surface area = 2 × (area of cross-section) + (perimeter of cross-section) × length
  • Cuboid 4 × 4 × 7: 2(4×4) + (4+4+4+4) × 7 = 32 + 112 = 144 cm².
  • Cube of side 9 cm: 6 × 9² = 486 cm²; volume 9³ = 729 cm³.
  • Cuboid l × w × h: TSA = 2(lw + lh + wh).
Surface area is measured in cm² (square units), not cm³.
