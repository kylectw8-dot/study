3A Algebraic expressions

Letters stand for numbers.

  • Terms are separated by + or −. x² − x + 9 has 3 terms.
  • The constant term has no letter (e.g. 9).
  • Like terms have exactly the same letters and powers: 2x and x are like terms; x² and x are not.

Simplifying

  • Collect like terms: 2a − 3b + a = 3a − 3b
  • 5x − 2y + y − 8x = −3x − y
  • −x − y + 3x − 4y = 2x − 5y
  • 2p × 3p = 6p²
  • Trapezium with parallel sides 2b and 3b, height h: area = (2b + 3b)h ÷ 2 = 5bh/2.
  • 6 fruit tarts at $m and 8 pancakes at $n cost $(6m + 8n).

3B Substitution into formulas

Put the number in place of the letter.

  1. Write the formula.
  2. Replace each letter with its value — use brackets for negatives.
  3. Follow the order of operations.
E = 9 − e², e = −2 → E = 9 − (−2)² = 9 − 4 = 5
  • A = a − 2, a = 8 → 6
  • B = 6b + 3, b = 4 → 27
  • F = −f² + 4f − 6, f = 5 → −25 + 20 − 6 = −11
  • g = 6x − 5y, x = 2, y = −4 → 12 + 20 = 32
  • h = 5 + 3n, n = 30 → 95 m
  • D = 2000x − 5000, x = 8 → 11 000 (a profit of $11 000)
(−2)² = +4, but −f² with f = 5 is −(5²) = −25.

  • General term aₙ: put n = 1, 2, 3, …. aₙ = 5n − 1 → 4, 9, 14. aₙ = 8(n − 2) → −8, 0, 8.
  • Function: y = x² − 3x + 1, x = −2 → 4 + 6 + 1 = 11.
  • Dots pattern 5, 8, 11, … (+3 each time): 8th figure = 5 + 7 × 3 = 26.
  • aₙ₊₂ = aₙ₊₁ − aₙ, a₂ = −2, a₃ = 5 → a₄ = a₃ − a₂ = 5 − (−2) = 7.
