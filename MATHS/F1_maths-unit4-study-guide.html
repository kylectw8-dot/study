4A Solving simple equations

Do the same thing to both sides.

An equation has an equals sign. To solve it, find the value of the unknown that makes both sides equal.

EquationStepAnswer
x + 2 = 5− 2 both sidesx = 3
x − 4 = 8+ 4 both sidesx = 12
3x = 18÷ 3 both sidesx = 6
x/6 = 9× 6 both sidesx = 54
3x − 7 = 11+ 7, then ÷ 3x = 6
5 − x = 8− 5 → −x = 3x = −3

Unknowns on both sides

  1. Move the unknown terms to one side.
  2. Move the numbers to the other side (change sign when you move).
  3. Divide by the coefficient.
5a − 2 = 3a + 14 → 5a − 3a = 14 + 2 → 2a = 16 → a = 8
Check: put your answer back into the equation. 5(8) − 2 = 38 and 3(8) + 14 = 38 ✔

4B Equations with brackets and fractions

Expand or divide first.

  • Brackets: expand, or divide both sides by the number outside.
8(x − 2) = −56 → x − 2 = −7 → x = −5
  • −4(x − 6) = 28 → x − 6 = −7 → x = −1
  • −3(5 − x) = 12 → 5 − x = −4 → x = 9
  • Fractions: multiply both sides by the denominator. (x − 9)/2 = 4 → x − 9 = 8 → x = 17
  • 2(3m − 2) − 5 = 9 → 6m − 4 − 5 = 9 → 6m = 18 → m = 3
When multiplying out, multiply every term inside the bracket: −3(5 − x) = −15 + 3x.

4C Solving problems with equations

Let x be the unknown.

  1. Let x be the unknown (write it down!).
  2. Write an equation from the question.
  3. Solve it.
  4. Answer in a sentence with units.
A pen costs $4 more than a pencil. 5 pens cost $80.
Let $x be the price of a pencil. 5(x + 4) = 80 → x + 4 = 16 → x = 12.
The price of a pencil is $12.
  • Consecutive numbers: x and x + 1. Consecutive odd numbers: x and x + 2.
  • Sum 116, difference 18: let the larger be x → x + (x − 18) = 116 → x = 67.
  • "Twice the number of boys": boys x, girls 2x. x + 2x = 57 → 19 boys, 38 girls.
  • Red is 2/3 of blue: blue x, red 2x/3. x + 2x/3 = 80 → blue 48, red 32.
