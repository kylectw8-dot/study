1B Powers, prime factors, H.C.F. and L.C.M.

Break numbers into primes.

  • Powers: 5 × 5 × 5 = 5³ (base 5, index 3). So 5 × 3 × 5 × 5 = 3 × 5³.
  • Prime factorization: write a number as a product of primes. Use short division: 36 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 = 2² × 3².
How (using prime factors)
H.C.F. (highest common factor)Take the common primes with the smallest index
L.C.M. (lowest common multiple)Take all primes with the largest index
24 = 2³ × 3   80 = 2⁴ × 5
H.C.F. = 2³ = 8   L.C.M. = 2⁴ × 3 × 5 = 240
  • 63 = 3² × 7, 84 = 2² × 3 × 7 → H.C.F. = 3 × 7 = 21, L.C.M. = 2² × 3² × 7 = 252.
  • Short division for 12 and 20: divide by common primes 2, 2 → left 3 and 5. H.C.F. = 2 × 2 = 4; L.C.M. = 2 × 2 × 3 × 5 = 60.
  • 8, 12, 15 → H.C.F. = 1, L.C.M. = 120.
H.C.F. is always ≤ the smallest number. L.C.M. is always ≥ the largest number.

1C Order of operations

Brackets first!

  1. Brackets ( ) first
  2. Then × and ÷, from left to right
  3. Then + and −, from left to right
4 × (9 + 1) − 8 = 4 × 10 − 8 = 40 − 8 = 32

Words to symbols

  • "Subtract 5 from 16" → 16 − 5 = 11 (order flips!)
  • "Add the product of 10 and 4 to 12" → 12 + 10 × 4 = 52
  • sum = +, difference = −, product = ×, quotient = ÷

Fractions

  • Add/subtract: use a common denominator.
  • Multiply: top × top, bottom × bottom.
  • Divide: multiply by the reciprocal (flip the second fraction).
2/3 + 1/4 = 8/12 + 3/12 = 11/12
