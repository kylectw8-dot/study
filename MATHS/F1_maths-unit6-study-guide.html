6B Polynomials: terms, degree, order

What counts as a polynomial?

  • A polynomial has terms where the letters have whole-number (non-negative) indices and are not in a denominator. 10, 2a²b, 3x/4 are polynomials; 4/y⁵ and 1/k³ are not.
  • Degree of a term = sum of the indices of its letters: 0.3pq³ has degree 1 + 3 = 4, coefficient 0.3.
  • Degree of a polynomial = the highest degree of its terms: −3m²n + 5mn⁴ − 1 has 3 terms and degree 5.
  • The constant term has no letter.

Arranging terms

  • 3x³ + 6 − 2x⁴ − x in ascending powers of x: 6 − x + 3x³ − 2x⁴
  • In descending powers of x: −2x⁴ + 3x³ − x + 6
Each term keeps its own sign when you move it.

Value of a polynomial

10 + 4x³ + 5x⁵ − 3x² + 2x when x = −1:
10 + 4(−1) + 5(−1) − 3(1) + 2(−1) = 10 − 4 − 5 − 3 − 2 = −4
  • When x = 1: 10 + 4 + 5 − 3 + 2 = 18.

6C Adding and subtracting polynomials

Collect like terms.

  • Only like terms can be combined: 6m − 9 − 4m = 2m − 9; 5x² − 3 − 2x² + 1 = 3x² − 2.
  • xy and yx are like terms; x²y and yx² are like terms.
  • Adding: remove the brackets, signs stay the same. (2x + 5) + (3x − 11) = 5x − 6
  • Subtracting: change every sign in the second bracket. (4x + 8) − (1 + x) = 4x + 8 − 1 − x = 3x + 7
(10x² + 8x + 20) − (−x² − 3x − 5) = 10x² + 8x + 20 + x² + 3x + 5 = 11x² + 11x + 25
A common mistake is changing only the first sign in the bracket. Change them all!

6D Multiplying and expanding

Every term times every term.

  • Multiply the outside term by each term inside: 5(x − 5) = 5x − 25; −2(x + 8) = −2x − 16.
  • −3y(6y − 5) = −18y² + 15y; 9y(8 − 2y) = 72y − 18y².

Two brackets

(x + 3)(x + 5) = x² + 5x + 3x + 15 = x² + 8x + 15
  • (x − 8)(x + 3) = x² + 3x − 8x − 24 = x² − 5x − 24
  • (3h − 1)(h + 5) = 3h² + 15h − h − 5 = 3h² + 14h − 5
  • (3m + 8)² = (3m + 8)(3m + 8) = 9m² + 48m + 64
(3m + 8)² is not 9m² + 64. Don't forget the middle term 2 × 3m × 8 = 48m.
